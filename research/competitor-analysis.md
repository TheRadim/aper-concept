# Competitor & reference analysis

Not a design catalogue — principles to borrow. Observations are from my knowledge of these brands' sites plus the 2026 press found during research; re-check before quoting anything to APER.

## Direct: machined-aluminium frames

**Pole Bicycles (Finland)** — the obvious comparison. CNC-machined 7075 halves bonded together (the "Machine"). Communicated engineering brilliantly (geometry-first, open about bonding) but **went bankrupt in 2024** and is now attempting a return (Singletracks 2026).
- Lesson for APER: buyers of machined frames from small makers *will* think "what if the company disappears?" APER's site must answer this head-on: in-house manufacturing, 5-year warranty, crash replacement, spares sold openly, real people with names.
- Lesson: Pole's strength was that geometry and construction were the brand. APER can do the same without the bravado.

**Atherton Bikes (UK)** — additive titanium lugs + carbon tubes. Strong founder story (the Athertons), process video, size-specific tubes.
- Principle: show the manufacturing *sequence*, not adjectives.

## Boutique gravel / adventure

**Sour, Bombtrack, Fairlight, Mason** — gravel/touring brands that sell on intent and fit.
- Fairlight: superb at fit (size-specific geometry explanation, "which size am I?").
- Mason: build/spec pages are clear and complete; prices visible.
- Bombtrack/Sour: personality through photography of real use, not studio perfection.

## Premium component makers (CNC culture)

**Hope Technology (UK)**, **Industry Nine (US)**, **Cane Creek** — machined-part companies whose credibility comes from showing the factory floor, chips, machines, people. Hope's "made in Barnoldswick" is a brand pillar; APER can own "made in Patras" the same way. Note APER already specs Industry Nine wheels and uses Hope parts on the KOMPace SE — shared culture.

## Premium cycling websites (general)

**Specialized / Canyon / Cervélo** — conversion machines: configurators, size finders, comparison tables. Too much for APER, but two patterns are cheap and valuable: a **size finder** using published rider-height ranges, and a **build comparison** where price changes with spec.

## Extracted principles for APER

| Principle | Why it matters for APER |
|---|---|
| Process over adjectives | Their copy currently says "cutting-edge"; their photos show a machining centre. Lead with the machine. |
| Answer the small-brand risk | Pole proves the fear is rational. Warranty, crash replacement, spares, people. |
| Publish everything | APER already publishes prices + full specs + geometry — rare. Make it beautiful and comparable. |
| Fit confidence | Direct-to-consumer, no dealers: a size finder replaces the shop fitting. |
| Place as identity | Patras / Erymanthos is to APER what Barnoldswick is to Hope. |
| One flagship image | Every strong site leads with a single great photograph. APER needs one shoot. |
