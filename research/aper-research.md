## Products

### DRYAD — gravel / bikepacking (launched Bespoked Dresden, 11–13 Sep 2026)
- Frame: 7075-T6 aluminium closed-section profiles, extensively CNC-machined with **RibLogic™** integrated stiffening ribs, variable wall sections and deep undercuts. [V]
- Joining: mechanically interlocking interfaces + structural bonding + mechanical fastening (bolts). [V]
- **Monolithic fork**: steerer, crown and blades machined from one 7075-T6 billet — no welds, no bonded crown. [V] ~740 g [P]
- **Bushing-mounted seatstays**: small controlled movement to reduce high-frequency vibration; bushings bolted and replaceable. [V]/[P]
- Frame weight from ~1,700 g. [P] — not on APER's site.
- Six triple-M5 mounting zones. [V]
- Five sizes XS–XL; full geometry table published. [V]
- Ships with 45 mm Vittoria Terreno T90 tyres; **max tyre clearance not published**. [?]
- Priority: "structural integrity and durability over the lowest possible weight." [V]
- Photographed in gold finish (anodised? [?]) with a black fork, in front of a vertical machining centre.
- "Tunable" flex via rib design — press calls this "in theory". [P] Don't claim.

### KOMPace — 29" enduro, 170/160 mm
- Patented **Rising Pivot**: lower shock mount on a sliding carriage on a rail; rearward axle path up to 45 mm (APER claim vs ~25 mm typical high pivot). [V]/[P]
- 7075 aluminium machined shells joined into a hollow monocoque — **bonded** (BIKE) vs **welded** (Bikerumor 2023). [?] Confirm.
- Frame from 4,080 g; flip-chip; sizes S1–S3. [P]
- Frameset from €3,969; complete from €7,769; MY2026 Special Edition €12,969; non-black anodising +€300. [V]/[P]
- Requires rail lubrication after every ride/wash (FAQ). [V] — honest maintenance info worth surfacing.

## What makes APER genuinely different
1. **Machined, not welded or moulded.** Engineers who own the mill and write their own CAM. Rare at this price — the Dryad frameset (€1,245) is cheaper than most custom steel/ti.
2. **A one-piece machined fork** — visually and technically the most unusual part of the Dryad.
3. **Serviceable compliance** — a replaceable bushing instead of a carbon layup claim. Mechanically honest.
4. **Two bikes, one method.** Enduro suspension patent and a bikepacking frame share the same manufacturing language.
5. **Place.** Patras and the Erymanthos mountains; the boar logo is a real local myth, not a mascot.
