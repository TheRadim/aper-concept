Help and support

Support

Answers from the people who designed and machined your bike.

Shipping

How much is shipping?

Bikes and framesets ship free. Accessories ship at a flat rate: courier €7 or Box NOW locker €1 within Greece, courier €12 internationally.

Where do orders ship from?

Every order ships from our workshop in Patras, Greece.

When will my DRYAD arrive?

The DRYAD is on pre-order. First deliveries are planned from December 2026; we confirm your date when your build is scheduled.

Ordering

How does the DRYAD pre-order work?

A €400 deposit reserves your frame slot and locks the pre-order price. We confirm size and build with you before machining starts. You pay the balance when your bike is finished.

Which KOMPace options are there?

Frameset, complete bike or the MY2026 Special Edition, in sizes S1 to S3. Black anodising is standard; coloured anodising is €300 more.

Can I return or change my order?

Bikes and framesets are machined for each order, so we can't take them back or exchange them for another size, colour or model. Ask us anything before you order and we'll help you choose.

Warranty and crash replacement

What does the warranty cover?

Five years from the date of purchase on frames, swingarms, rails and every other frame part we manufacture, for the first owner of a bike or frameset bought from APER or an authorised reseller. If a defect is covered, we repair it or replace the part with an equivalent one, which may differ in colour.

What isn't covered?

Components from other brands (covered by their own manufacturers), normal wear, crash damage, incorrect assembly or maintenance, incompatible parts, cosmetic marks and rental use.

What is crash replacement?

A hard crash can damage a frame in ways that make it unsafe. For five years after purchase, we replace a damaged APER frame component at 35% off its price at the time you ask. The colour may differ; the part is fully functional and safe.

How do I make a claim?

Email info@aper-bikes.com with your order details, a description and photos. Please inspect your bike when it arrives and tell us about any visible defect straight away.

Care and parts

How should I look after my bike?

Clean the frame after riding. On the KOMPace, lubricate the Rising Pivot rail after every ride and every wash: a dry rail wears faster. For adjustments, visit a specialist workshop or ask us.

Where can I get spare parts?

Thru axles, hangers, idlers, pulleys, rail bearings and protection kits are in our accessories shop.
