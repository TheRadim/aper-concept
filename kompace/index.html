The bike that started APER

A 29-inch enduro frame built around a suspension idea from Nikos's thesis at the University of Patras.

Developed and ridden in the Erymanthos mountains above Patras. Machined from 7075 aluminium, with a flip-chip for two geometry settings.

Rising Pivot

The lower shock mount rides on a carriage that slides along a rail. Over an obstacle the rear axle can move back by up to 45 mm, by APER's figures, so the wheel gets out of the way instead of into it.

Maintenance: lubricate the Rising Pivot rail after every ride or wash.
