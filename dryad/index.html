Made for the road after the road ends

Forest tracks, broken tarmac, mountain passes. Loaded for a week, or stripped back for a fast day.

The DRYAD puts structural integrity and durability ahead of the lowest possible weight. Six triple-M5 mounting zones take bottles, cargo cages and bags. The name comes from the Greek drys, oak.

Features

How APER machines a frame

RibLogic frame

Each tube starts as a closed aluminium profile. The mill cuts integrated ribs, varies the wall thickness and undercuts deep pockets, leaving material where load travels.

One-piece fork

Steerer, crown and blades are machined from a single billet of 7075-T6. No bonded crown, no weld, no separate blades.

Seatstays on bushings

A small, controlled movement under load takes the sting out of washboard gravel without making the rear end vague. The bushings are bolted in and replaceable.

Questions

Anything else: ask the team.

How much does it weigh?

About 1,700 g for the frame and about 740 g for the fork, as reported at launch. The DRYAD puts durability and load capacity ahead of the lowest possible weight.

How wide can the tyres go?

Every build ships on 45 mm Vittoria Terreno T90. Maximum clearance will be published with the final specification.

What do the seatstay bushings do?

They let the seatstays move a small, controlled amount under load, reducing high-frequency vibration while keeping the rear end laterally stiff. They are bolted in and replaceable.

What does the warranty cover?

Frames and frame components carry a five-year warranty. If you damage a frame in a crash within five years, the Crash Replacement Program offers a discounted replacement.

Can I return it?

No. Each frame is built to order, so APER does not offer returns or cancellations. Ask any question before you reserve.
