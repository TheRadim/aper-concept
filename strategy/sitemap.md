## Decisions
- **Engineering as its own page *and* inside the Dryad page.** The Dryad page carries the 3 product-specific stories (ribs, fork, bushing) briefly; `/engineering/` is the long-form "how we machine a frame" story that serves both bikes and the brand. Keeps the product page scannable.
- **Rising Pivot folds into KOMPace.** It's a feature of one bike, not a destination.
- **Support replaces FAQ + Warranty + Shipping footer pages.** Trust content becomes one findable page and is excerpted next to every price.
- **Shop removed from main nav.** Spares are found from Support and bike pages.
