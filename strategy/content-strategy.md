# Content strategy

## Voice
Engineer explaining to a rider, across a workbench. Short declarative sentences. Numbers instead of adjectives. Say what a feature *does*, then what that means on a ride — and stop when the evidence stops.

**Write:** "The fork is one part. There is no crown joint to bond, weld or check."
**Don't write:** "Revolutionary monolithic technology for unmatched performance."

Banned: redefining, unleash, limitless, passion meets, cutting-edge, unparalleled, game-changing.

## Translating engineering into benefits (with evidence limits)
| Engineering fact [V] | What it enables | Claim limit |
|---|---|---|
| Members machined from 7075-T6 profile with ribs and variable walls | Material placed where load goes; geometry and stiffness can change per size without new moulds | Don't claim lighter/stiffer than X without test data |
| Interlock + bond + bolt joints | No heat-affected weld zones; parts made to tolerance before assembly | Don't claim "stronger than welded" |
| One-billet fork | No crown joint; steerer/crown/blades share one load path | Weight 740 g is press-reported — confirm |
| Bushing seatstays | Small controlled movement → less high-frequency buzz; replaceable wear part | Don't quantify comfort |
| Six triple-M5 zones | Bottles, cargo cages, frame bags | Rack/fender support unconfirmed |
| In-house design → CAM → machining → assembly | Short feedback loop; spares made by the same people | — |
| 5-yr warranty + crash replacement | Risk reduction | Terms per Support page |
