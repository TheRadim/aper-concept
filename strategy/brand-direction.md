# Brand direction

## Positioning (one line)
**APER machines bicycles in Patras.** Two engineers who own the mill, write the toolpaths and stand behind every part — at a price that makes custom-level construction reachable.

Not "innovation", not "premium". *Made, and explained.*

## The idea that runs the concept
> On this site, **the RibLogic pocket — a triangle whose corners are rounded to the radius of the end mill that cut it** — becomes **the mask for every photograph, the shape of the primary button and the marker in every list.**

Every inside corner of a machined part has a radius, because a round cutter can't make a sharp inside corner. It's the fingerprint of machining. Pole, a welder or a carbon brand can't claim it in the same way; APER's ribbed frames and their triangulated fork are made of exactly these pockets.

## Type
**Archivo** (variable weight + width, OFL). Display set semi-condensed (wdth ~75, wght 600, tracking −0.03em) — the stamped, engineering-drawing feel without a second face. Body at normal width, 17–19 px. One family only. Tabular figures for every number.
